Accessible topology

How does elevator availability change the accessible topology of Berlin public transport over time?

This page measures one thing: hours during which a station lift was reported out of service, and how much of each day we were actually watching. It is not a measure of punctuality, frequency, crowding, or service quality, and it cannot support a claim about those.

Unit: outage-hours. Days are Europe/Berlin local days, which are 23 or 25 hours long twice a year. Storage is UTC.

Source: BrokenLifts (community accessibility data). Method: event schema · retention
