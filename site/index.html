Collection paused · 10 July 2026

Transit Friction

The former live dashboard has been withdrawn. Its event counts, rankings, and trends were generated by a prototype whose event identity and aggregation model were not valid measures of Berlin transit reliability.

What happens now

Scheduled collectors are disabled and the historical output is preserved only as a legacy research artifact. It should not be used for claims about service quality.

Proposed reboot

The project is being narrowed to a testable accessibility question:

How does elevator availability change the accessible topology of Berlin public transport over time?

Collection will resume only after stable asset identity, persistent lifecycle state, explicit coverage, and a reviewed shadow run are in place.

Repository and assessment · Public data only · No passenger tracking · No fabricated precision
